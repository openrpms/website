Users & use cases

RPMS is used by everyone in a clinic, from the front desk to the pharmacy to the billing office. This page names those people, and what each of them needs the system to do.

Each role is a job at a clinic, not a single person. At a small site one person often does several. The stories are written the way the work is described: as a role, I want something, so that something good happens.
